## Goal of the session (user)

Make the RGM over H-JEPA more general: no fixed 1/16 threshold, no fixed number of states, and a real role for
epistemic value; the map (the graph) built by playing + imagining with curiosity, not offline. Routes: "short is good
but probable is also good".

## What was built — `system2/rgm_curious.py` (`CuriousRGM`, on top of `LiveRGM`)

- **Hop model instead of 1/16.** Each hop s -> t the reflex is asked for succeeds per latent step with p ~ Beta.
  Prior q from real passive transitions; for dream-only edges, a LEARNED precision of imagination (below).
- **Risk = -ln V**, V = E[lambda^arrival] of reaching the goal within the latent steps LEFT in the episode (value
  iteration over the hop model). Short and probable both count; the deadline decides between them
  (toy check: 6 steps left -> the probable 2-hop route; 1 step left -> the risky direct hop).
- **Novelty** = expected information gain of a try about p (Beta-Bernoulli mutual information, closed form). With a
  goal: score = ln q + eta * IG(next hop). No goal (explore phase) or no route yet: discounted IG along routes (goes
  to the frontier). Replaces the induction + EFE-fallback cascade.
- **Imagination is a hypothesis, reality decides.** Dreaming the same move again adds no evidence. The trust in a
  dreamed hop = rate at which reality confirmed SIMILAR dreamed hops (Gaussian kernel on start and end), per rollout
  depth (1, 2, 3+; depth d starts at g1^d), shrunk to the map-wide rate. Walls refute dreams; no refusal rule.
- States still grow by radius (`rho` = 0.5 F3 steps; was 1.0). Data-decided states are NOT done yet.
- Constants left: kappa (prior strength), kernel_w, eta, lambda (time preference), gamma, rho.

Runner: `eval_l3_planner.py --rgm-curious [--rgm-explore] [--rgm-explorer curious|random] [--rgm-dream]
--rgm-eta --rgm-rho --rgm-persist map.pkl`. Experiment driver: `experiments/run_curious.py` (empty map -> explore B
episodes -> snapshot -> goal tasks, per budget). Report: `experiments/curious_report.py` (map size, tested /
confirmed / refuted edges, maze coverage via the xy probe, success).

## Toy check — `experiments/test_rgm_curious.py` (numpy only)

Square with a wall, gap at the top; imagination ignores the wall 25% (≈ F3's error) or 100% of the time; reflex
blocked by the wall. 10 explore episodes x 17 steps, then start -> goal behind the wall, 40 steps, 5 seeds:

| imagination through walls | explorer | sleep-dreaming | goal solved | mean steps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25% | curious | no | 5/5 | 18.2 |
| 25% | random | no | 0/5 | – |
| 100% | curious | no | 5/5 | 19.0 |
| 100% | random | no | 0/5 | – |
| 25% / 100% | curious or random | yes | 0/5 | – |

- Curiosity is what makes the map usable: it tests dreamed edges during exploration (23–24 wall edges tried vs 1–7
  for random), so the goal phase trusts the right ones. Random exploration covers as much space (coverage 0.87–0.90)
  but leaves dreams untested.
- **Known failure: sleep-dreaming (F3 rollouts) makes it worse.** The map fills with dreamed states and shortcuts
  through the wall faster than exploration can test them; the goal phase spends its time testing them. Fixes tried
  that helped the failure analysis but not the result: dream evidence counted once, per-source then kernel trust,
  per-depth trust with compounding prior, lambda 0.8–0.98. Not tried yet: test dreams in imagination first (F3
  round-trip / cycle consistency), cap untested dreams per region, or dream only where reality has been.
- Bugs found on the way: repeated dreams were counted as new evidence (outweighed real failures); a failed hop
  through a wall only refuted that one edge (fixed by the kernel).

## Simulator smoke (AntMaze, seed 42)

`run_curious.py --arm smoke_curious --explorer curious --budgets 0 2 --explore-tasks 0 1 --eval-tasks 0 1`
(no sleep-dreaming; log `logs/curious_smoke.log`, results `results/curious/seed42/smoke_curious/`). The wiring works
end to end (explore episodes, map carried in a pickle, goal tasks, report). Results are NOT good yet:

| phase | map before | goal tasks solved | hops ok / failed per episode | decisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| goal, budget 0 (empty map) | – | 0/2 | 2–4 / 9–11 | 14 epistemic, 0 pragmatic |
| explore, 2 episodes | 4 → 11 real states, coverage 12.5% | (task 1 hit its goal by chance at 336) | 2–5 / 8–11 | 14 epistemic |
| goal, budget 2 | 11 real states | 0/2 | 3–4 / 9–10 | 14 epistemic, 0 pragmatic |

Two problems, both clear from the stats:
1. **The goal is never on the map** (0 pragmatic decisions: no route has any probability). Without sleep-dreaming the
   goal state only gets edges when one-step imagination from the live latent lands next to it. Fix: imagine one F3
   step FROM the goal latent at task start (and its reverse edges), so the goal is linked to its neighbourhood.
2. **~70% of requested hops fail.** Likely cause: targets are imagined states whose exemplar is an F3 latent, and
   H-JEPA reliably reaches only real encodings (19 Nov finding). To check: success rate of hops to real vs imagined
   targets (decisions in the task JSONs + `real` flags in the map pickles). Possible fixes: prefer real targets
   (exemplar = nearest real latent when one exists), or let level 2 see the target for 2 latent steps before
   counting a failure.
Measured on the smoke: hops to imagined targets 0/18, to real targets 14/34; hops longer than 2 state radii 0/14.

**Fixes (same day)**: `link_goal` (one F3 step imagined from the goal latent at task start and every 4 latent
steps, edges both ways); imagined targets get a FRESH one-step F3 latent from now (`fresh_f3_targets`); a hop counts
as made within one radius of the target's centre (`hit_radius`). Toy test still passes at 25% hallucination (4–5/5);
at 100% hallucination the fresh-F3 target is itself a hallucination and the curious arm drops to 0/5 (toy only: its
reflex reaches any latent, so it cannot show H-JEPA's reachability problem).

Smoke 2 (`smoke2_curious`, same protocol, `logs/curious_smoke2.log`):

| phase | goal tasks solved | hops ok / failed (per episode) | decisions |
|---|---|---|---|
| goal, budget 0 | 0/2 | 5 / 8, 7 / 6 | all epistemic |
| explore, 2 episodes | – | 4 / 9, 7 / 6 | all epistemic |
| goal, budget 2 | **1/2 (task 1 in 259 steps)** | 3 / 10, 2 / 11 | task 0 all epistemic; task 1 all pragmatic |

Hop success rose from ~26% to ~36% over the six episodes, and the first goal-driven solve appeared (task 1 after 2
explore episodes). Task 0 still never gets a route (goal linked, but not to the part of the map the agent is in).
Four tasks is far too few to call any of this; it only shows the pieces work.

Also: only 14 RGM decisions per 350-step episode (level 3 is consulted every 20 env steps and not after ~270),
so 2 explore episodes = 28 decisions -- the budgets in the real experiment need to be larger (25–100 episodes).

## Screen: explore, then goals (AntMaze tasks 0–12, seed 42, one run each)

`run_curious.py --arm screen_<explorer> --explorer <curious|random> --budgets 0 10 25 --eval-tasks 0..12`, no
sleep-dreaming, both arms in parallel on one GPU (~1.5 h). Explore episodes start from the start states of tasks
0–24 (their goals ignored). Each budget's goal tasks run in order on a copy of that budget's map, which keeps
learning from task to task (so budget 0 is "learn while solving", not "no map"). Logs `logs/curious_screen_*.log`,
results `results/curious/seed42/screen_*`.

| explorer | explore episodes | states (real) | edges | tried / confirmed / refuted | maze coverage | solved | mean steps | hop success |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| curious | 0 | – | – | – | – | 5/13 | 241 | 26% |
| curious | 10 | 89 (38) | 281 | 112 / 37 / 57 | 35% | 5/13 | 251 | 24% |
| curious | 25 | 139 (80) | 630 | 289 / 109 / 68 | 62% | **9/13** | 244 | 31% |
| random | 0 | – | – | – | – | 4/13 | 184 | 44% |
| random | 10 | 131 (53) | 506 | 101 / 47 / 2 | 46% | 5/13 | 232 | 27% |
| random | 25 | 194 (100) | 958 | 227 / 82 / 74 | 67% | **8/13** | 248 | 20% |

Per task (S = solved), tasks 0..12:
curious B0 `.....S.S.S.SS`, B10 `.S...S..S.S.S`, B25 `S.S.SS.SSSS.S`;
random B0 `.....S...SSS.`, B10 `.S...S.SS...S`, B25 `SSS.SSS...S.S`.

What this does and does not show:
- **A map built from nothing, by playing and imagining, works.** Success rises with exploration for both explorers
  (4–5/13 -> 8–9/13 after 25 episodes = 8,750 env steps). For scale, on 20 Nov (older package versions, NOT
  re-run here) the offline map from 1,250 recorded episodes solved 13/13, 13/13, 11/13 on these tasks and H-JEPA's
  own planner 9/13.
- **Curious vs random is not separated** (9/13 vs 8/13, one seed, single runs that are known to be noisy). Their
  maps differ: random covers a bit more maze (67% vs 62%) with half again as many edges, curious tests a larger share
  of its edges (289 of 630 vs 227 of 958). Whether that matters needs more seeds and a larger budget.
- **With a map, the goal drives behaviour**: pragmatic decisions grow with the budget (curious 89 -> 164 of ~175;
  random 48 -> 132), so the risk planner has routes to use.
- **Hops are still the bottleneck: 20–31% of requested hops succeed after exploring.** Most failures are probably
  hops to imagined states and hops longer than the reflex makes in one latent step.
