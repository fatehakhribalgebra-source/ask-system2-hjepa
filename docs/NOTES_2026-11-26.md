## The first life (seed 42, 7000 play steps, 20 goals, 350 steps each): 3/20

`python experiments/life_run.py --seed 42 --play-steps 7000 --goals 20 --out results/life/seed42` (1 h 25 min,
sharing the GPU with the queue). Plot: `results/life/seed42/life_seed42.png`.

Reached goals 0, 4, 5 (all in the first six); nothing after step ~8850. Goals do **not** get easier as the life goes on.

What the trace shows:

1. **Play does not travel.** In 7000 steps of curious play the ant stays within about 3 units of its birth place
   (xy spread 0.5-0.9 per 700-step window, the grey blob at the bottom right). The map grows to 149 states, but only
   35 are real; the rest are dreamed. Half of the epistemic moves fail (about 6 of 13 per bout). Curiosity is satisfied
   locally: dreaming creates new states near home faster than the ant can go and check them.
2. **Goal pursuit is all RGM, all failing.** No handovers and no "no route" in any goal bout: the RGM always finds a
   route, but most moves fail (0-2 of 13 succeed per bout from goal 6 on). The routes run through dreamed states that
   were never visited. Confirmed moves stop growing at ~136 while dreamed states keep growing to 446.
3. **Stuck from step ~9800.** The ant ends at about (9, 16): path length per 700 steps halves and xy spread drops to
   0.1-0.3, so the legs move but the body does not (wedged on a wall, or flipped over; the trace only has xy, so we
   can't tell). With no resets nothing gets it out, and goals 9-19 are lost there. Goal 12 started only 2.6 away and
   was still missed.

Ideas this suggests (not decided):
- Log the torso height and orientation in `life_trace.npz` so stuck vs flipped can be told apart, and count steps with
  no displacement.
- Plan only over real (or confirmed) states during goal pursuit; use dreamed states for curiosity only.
- Make play travel: an epistemic value for unvisited *real* places, or a cap on dreamed states per real state.
- A "stuck" reflex (e.g. a random bout of low-level actions after N steps with no displacement) before thinking.

## Queue results (`experiments/queue_2026_11_26.py`, log `logs/queue_2026-11-26.log`, all 12 jobs done)

### Full protocol: the curious play map is well below the offline map

| Map (K=64, fewest hops) | seed 42 | seed 43 | seed 44 | mean ± SE | RGM decides |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| curious play, 50 explore episodes | 30/50 | 24/50 | 23/50 | **51.3 ± 4.4** | 23 / 62 / 42 % |
| offline dataset (committed 20 Nov) | 35/50 | 42/50 | 37/50 | **76.0 ± 4.2** | 78 / 84 / 86 % |

Task by task, offline-only wins outnumber play-only wins 13-8, 19-1 and 18-4. The 24 Nov "13/13 on tasks 0-12" with the
same seed-42 map (rebuilt today from the committed explore dumps) came out 7/13 today: that result was mostly noise
plus H-JEPA handovers.

Connectivity (`map_connectivity.py`, any edge): seed 42 18/64 strongly connected (23% of pairs reachable), seed 43
56/64 (80%), seed 44 30/64 (35%); offline 56/64 (88%). Seed 43's play map is about as connected as the offline map and
the RGM decides 62% of the time, yet it scores 48% vs 84%: connectivity is not the only problem; the play map's
states/edges are worse (play covers less of the maze around the tasks, and edges counted from play are less reliable).

- **Imagined counts hurt, the same way dreaming hurts the life**: the RGM becomes confident (decides 90%) on hops that
  were never tried, and success falls 11 -> 4. Three results now agree (24 Nov sleep-dreaming, today's imagined
  counts, the life run): imagination added to the map as if it were experience makes the planner worse.
- Auto K (128) fragments the play map further (25/128) and gives nothing over K=64.
- The risk planner is the one thing that helps on both maps (play 7 -> 11, offline 8 -> 11 on the screen).
